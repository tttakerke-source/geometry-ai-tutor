Your Geometry Challenge

A right triangle has legs of 3 cm and 4 cm. Find the length of the hypotenuse.

♧ Hint

For a right triangle, use the Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c². The hypotenuse is the longest side, opposite the right angle.

✦ Step-by-Step Solution

  1. Write the formula: a² + b² = c².
  2. Substitute the known side lengths: 3² + 4² = c².
  3. Calculate: 9 + 16 = 25, so c = √25 = 5 cm.
Answer

The hypotenuse is 5 cm.

▤ Quick Explanation

The Pythagorean theorem connects the two shorter sides of a right triangle to its hypotenuse. Squaring the legs and adding them gives the square of the hypotenuse.
